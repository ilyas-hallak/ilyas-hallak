### Hi there 👋

I'm Ilyas, a Senior Software Engineer from Bremen.
Most of my time goes into iOS apps with Swift and SwiftUI, with distributed backends, DevOps and lately agentic AI engineering on the side.
For a few years I've also been organising [CocoaHeads Bremen](https://www.eventbrite.com/o/cocoaheads-bremen-57377670733), a meetup for everyone who builds for Apple platforms.

In my spare time I build open source apps for the self-hosted tools I run at home:

- [**RomM for iOS**](https://github.com/ilyas-hallak/romm-ios-app), play your self-hosted [RomM](https://github.com/rommapp/romm) retro library on iPhone and iPad, saves synced with your server
- [**Readeck for iOS**](https://github.com/ilyas-hallak/readeck-ios), a native client for the [Readeck](https://readeck.org) read-it-later service, out on the App Store
- [**Readeck for Safari**](https://github.com/ilyas-hallak/readeck-safari-extension), save pages to Readeck straight from Safari
